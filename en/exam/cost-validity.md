# Costs, Validity & CPD

"How much does CCISO cost, and how long is it valid?" are fair questions — but the honest answer is *check the issuer today*. This independent, unofficial article lists **cost components** and the **questions to ask**, without inventing prices or CPD numbers. EC-Council states that policies may change without notice. Verify on [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/certified-chief-information-security-officer-cciso/), [cert.eccouncil.org](https://cert.eccouncil.org/), and [ciso.eccouncil.org/cciso-certification](https://ciso.eccouncil.org/cciso-certification/). Credential names are trademarks of their owners; this site contains no official courseware or exam dumps.

## Cost components, not price tags

| Component | What to ask before paying |
|---|---|
| Training or courseware | Is it required for my route? Who is the provider, and what is included? |
| Exam voucher | Is eligibility confirmed first? Which delivery mode and region applies? |
| Labs and case resources | Do I need practice environments or legitimate practice exams? |
| Proctoring and delivery | Are there separate online-proctoring fees or local charges? |
| Renewal / CPD | What current obligations apply after certification? |
| Optional costs | Travel, study time, professional memberships, employer reimbursement rules |

The official certification site makes a **free-market statement**: pricing can vary by region, partner, and proctoring arrangement. Get a written quote that lists what is included, and compare like with like. Never rely on a price copied from an old blog or a forum screenshot. Confirm your route with [Eligibility: Full CCISO & Associate Path](eligibility.md) **before** buying a voucher: official e-courseware is study material only, not proof of eligibility.

## Validity, renewal, and CPD

Certification validity, renewal cycles, continuing professional education (CPD) requirements, and the accepted ways to earn credits are all issuer policies that can change. We deliberately do **not** state fixed numbers. On the official certification pages, look for the current policy version and its effective date; save a copy. Then set your own reminder to re-check before any deadline. Keeping a personal log of learning activities, dates, and evidence is useful regardless of the exact rule.

## Retakes and result policies

Retake rules, waiting periods, fees, and result reporting are also subject to change. Read the current policy from EC-Council rather than trusting second-hand summaries. If an exam does not go as hoped, use the [CCISO Exam Format & Blueprint](exam-format.md) workflow to rebuild your weak-domain list — and use legitimate practice resources only.

## Accreditation claims

EC-Council states that its certifications are accredited under **ANAB** against **ISO/IEC 17024** requirements. Treat this as an official claim to verify on current official pages, not as a guarantee of a particular job, salary, or recognition in every country. Employer and regulator acceptance varies; check local requirements.

## Budget worksheet checklist

- [ ] My chosen route is confirmed on official pages.
- [ ] I have a dated quote with region, provider, and delivery mode.
- [ ] I separated required costs from optional ones.
- [ ] I noted current renewal/CPD and retake policies with their effective dates.
- [ ] I understand that vendor survey figures are marketing claims, not guaranteed returns.

This is education, **not financial, tax, legal, or career advice**. Taxes, employer reimbursement, and professional recognition differ by country; consult qualified professionals and official sources before making commitments.
