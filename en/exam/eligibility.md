# Eligibility: Full CCISO & Associate Path

This article explains CCISO eligibility routes in plain English. It is an **independent, unofficial study resource**: we are not affiliated with or endorsed by EC-Council, and we publish no official courseware or exam dumps. CCISO, CISSP, CISM, CISA and other credential names are trademarks of their respective owners. Confirm your own route on official pages before paying anything.

## Verify before you apply

The current official branding is **CCISO v4 — AI-Enhanced** (checked 26 November 2026). EC-Council states that policies may change without notice, so treat the following as a starting map, not a contract. Check [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/certified-chief-information-security-officer-cciso/), [cert.eccouncil.org](https://cert.eccouncil.org/), and the [CCISO certification page](https://ciso.eccouncil.org/cciso-certification/) for the current rules. Keep a dated copy.

## Full CCISO: experience across domains

| Official expectation (verify current wording) | Beginner translation |
|---|---|
| 5+ years of information security experience | Years alone are not the whole story; the *content* of the work matters. |
| Experience in at least 3 of the 5 domains | The five areas are Govern, Lead, Operate, Secure, and Strategize. Breadth matters. |
| Training candidates demonstrate 3-of-5 domain experience via the application | Expect to describe real responsibilities, not just list job titles. |

For the full credential, EC-Council states an expectation of **5+ years** with experience in **at least 3 of 5 domains**. For candidates who complete official training, the application is where you must **demonstrate experience in 3 of the 5 domains**. EC-Council decides what evidence is acceptable; keep position descriptions, project records, and a short domain mapping you can defend honestly.

## Associate CCISO path

The Associate route is designed for people earlier in the journey. Official information describes **2+ years of experience in one domain**, **or** holding **CISSP, CISM, or CISA** (verify the accepted list and current wording). An Associate credential is a distinct step — it is not the same as the full CCISO. If you plan to progress, ask the issuer what conditions currently apply rather than assuming automatic conversion.

## Academia path

Students may find a route through an **EC-Council Academia Partner**: officially described as being a student at such a partner, having **30+ post-secondary credit hours**, and using the **official CCISO Academia Series courseware** (verify all three points with the school and EC-Council). Availability varies by institution and country.

## Order of operations

1. Read the official pages and the current exam blueprint.
2. Map your experience to the five domains.
3. Confirm which route fits you and submit the eligibility application.
4. **Apply for eligibility before purchasing an exam voucher.**
5. Only then budget for training, practice resources, and the exam.

Official e-courseware is **study material only** — buying it does not prove eligibility. The exam blueprint is recommended reading because it shows what the exam is designed to measure. See [CCISO Exam Format & Blueprint](exam-format.md) for how to use it.
