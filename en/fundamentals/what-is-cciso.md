# What Is CCISO v4

**CCISO** stands for **Certified Chief Information Security Officer**, a credential from EC-Council for leaders who develop and lead an organization's cybersecurity strategy. The current official branding is **CCISO v4 — AI-Enhanced** (checked on EC-Council's site on 26 Nov 2026).

This is an independent, unofficial study resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by EC-Council, and it contains no official courseware or exam dumps. EC-Council, CCISO, CISSP, CISM, and CISA are trademarks of their respective owners.

## What kind of certification is it?

Think of CCISO as a **leadership** credential, not an entry-level technical one. Where a hands-on security course might ask "how do I configure this control?", CCISO asks "how do I decide which risks matter, fund the right program, and explain the plan to the board?"

The program is organized around five executive domains: **Govern, Lead, Operate, Secure, and Strategize**. In v4, AI-integrated governance and risk themes are woven in — for example, how AI changes risk ownership and how leaders oversee automated decisions. Because branding and content evolve, always verify the current version on [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/certified-chief-information-security-officer-cciso/) and [cert.eccouncil.org](https://cert.eccouncil.org/).

## The eight skill areas, in plain language

EC-Council describes the program through eight broad areas. Paraphrased, they cover:

| Area | What a beginner should picture |
| --- | --- |
| Security governance and strategy | Rules, roles, and long-term direction |
| Risk, compliance, and GRC | Deciding what to accept, treat, or report |
| AI, automation, emerging tech | New tools and the risks they introduce |
| Leadership, ethics, executive presence | Influencing people and acting with integrity |
| Finance and vendor governance | Budgets, procurement, and supplier oversight |
| Security operations | SOC, incidents, and useful metrics |
| Technical and architecture foundations | Enough depth to challenge technical claims |
| Privacy, awareness, and culture | Helping everyone make safer choices |

You do not need to master every area on day one. The point is to understand how they connect: a firewall decision is also a budget decision, a compliance decision, and a communication decision.

## Who is it aimed at?

Typically: security managers, IT leaders, risk and governance professionals, and consultants moving into a **CISO** or deputy CISO role. EC-Council's published eligibility expects roughly **5+ years of experience**, with exposure to at least **3 of the 5 domains**; the Associate route is described as **2+ years in one domain** or holding **CISSP/CISM/CISA**. Treat these as the issuer's stated criteria and verify the full rules at the [CCISO certification page](https://ciso.eccouncil.org/cciso-certification/) before applying. See [Eligibility: Full CCISO & Associate Path](../exam/eligibility.md).

## Claims worth checking

EC-Council states that the program is ANAB accredited, aligned with ISO/IEC 17024 for personnel certification, and approved under US DoD 8570/8140. Check the current status with the official accreditation and government bodies — approvals and lists change.

The official page also publishes numbers on salary growth, job roles, recommendation rates, and board risk statistics. These are **vendor-published survey or marketing claims** with their own methodology, not guarantees or independent market data. This article is education, not legal, financial, tax, or regulatory advice.

## Verify before you act

Version names, eligibility, exam policy, pricing, and renewal rules change. Always verify on [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/certified-chief-information-security-officer-cciso/) and [cert.eccouncil.org](https://cert.eccouncil.org/) before spending time or money.
